- **13% weighting.** Installing and configuring laptops and mobile devices — a hands-on-experience domain given how physically different laptop repair is from desktop repair

## Laptop hardware and components

- **Internal component replacement**: knowing the location, function, and safe replacement procedure for battery, keyboard, RAM (SODIMM form factor specifically), HDD/SSD, and wireless cards
- **Storage migration and cloning**: understanding what migrating or cloning a drive means when upgrading a laptop's storage, and the difference between HDD (mechanical, more fragile, slower) and SSD (flash-based, faster, no moving parts)
- **Wi-Fi antenna handling**: correctly identifying and safely handling wireless card antenna connections during laptop disassembly — a frequently tested physical-handling detail

## Mobile device displays and accessories

- **Display technologies**: comparing LCD variants (IPS, TN, VA) and OLED — viewing angle, color accuracy, and power consumption trade-offs between them
- **Accessories and connectivity**: common mobile device accessories and how they connect (wired vs. wireless), including docking stations and port replicators for laptops

## Mobile device security features

- Basic awareness of biometric security features (fingerprint, face recognition) and NFC for physical device security and secure payments — recognized at an identification level, with deeper security policy treatment reserved for Core 2's Security domain
