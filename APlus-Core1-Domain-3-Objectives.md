- **25% weighting — the second-heaviest Core 1 domain.** Core desktop/server hardware — identifying, installing, and configuring the physical components a support technician handles daily

## Core system components

- **Motherboards, CPUs, and RAM**: form factors, socket types, and compatibility considerations when selecting or replacing components
- **Storage devices**: HDD, SSD, and their interfaces (SATA, NVMe) — matching the right storage type and interface to a given performance/cost requirement

## Power and cooling

- **Power supplies**: wattage requirements, connector types, and safe installation/replacement procedures
- **Cooling solutions**: air cooling vs. liquid cooling basics, and recognizing symptoms of inadequate cooling (thermal throttling, unexpected shutdowns)

## Peripherals and expansion

- **Common peripherals and their connections**: printers, external storage, and input/output devices, along with their typical connector/port types
- **Expansion cards and multimedia devices**: installing and configuring add-in cards (e.g., graphics cards) and multimedia peripherals

## Custom PC builds and multifunction devices

- **Custom configurations**: recognizing hardware requirements for specific use cases (e.g., a workstation optimized for graphics work vs. a basic office PC)
- **Printers and multifunction devices**: installation, configuration, and basic maintenance — a recurring practical support scenario topic
