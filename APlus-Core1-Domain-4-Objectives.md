## Client-side virtualization

- **Purpose and use cases of virtual machines**: running a VM on a client device for testing, compatibility, or isolation purposes — recognizing when virtualization is the appropriate support solution for a described scenario
- **Resource requirements**: understanding that VMs share and consume the host system's CPU, RAM, and storage resources, and how to plan for adequate host capacity

## Cloud computing concepts for support technicians

- **Cloud-connected devices and printers**: an increasing share of support work involves cloud-managed hardware (printers, endpoint management platforms) rather than fully local, standalone devices — recognizing the practical support implications (e.g., troubleshooting an internet-dependent cloud printer differently than a locally-networked one)
- **General cloud service awareness**: recognizing basic cloud service concepts (already introduced conceptually in Tech+) applied here to real support scenarios, such as troubleshooting connectivity to a cloud-hosted resource
